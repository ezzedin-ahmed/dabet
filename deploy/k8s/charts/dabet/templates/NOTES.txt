Health (docs §4.5)
  /healthz  process alive   -> liveness probe
  /readyz   can serve       -> readiness probe

  A moderation-path service that has lost a dependency STAYS READY. It fails
  open and keeps consuming (P2). If you are here because a dependency is down
  and the pods still show Ready, that is the design working, not a bug — look
  at fail_open_total and kafka_consumer_lag_messages instead.

Quick checks
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "dabet.fullname" . }}-moderation-service 9090:9090
  curl -s localhost:9090/metrics | grep -E 'fail_open_total|kafka_consumer_lag_messages'

  fail_open_total is the single most important metric in the system (§4.5).
  It must be zero in steady state.
